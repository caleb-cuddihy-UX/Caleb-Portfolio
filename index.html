How I work

Research first.
Design second. Always.

01
Understand the real problem
Every project starts with research — interviews, observations, and synthesis to understand what users actually need. I build empathy before I open Figma. The problem you start with is rarely the problem you end up solving.
02
Define, frame & ideate
HMW statements, personas, information architecture, and prioritised feature sets. I move from insight to direction with structured creativity — lo-fi before hi-fi, and always willing to throw away a bad idea early.
03
Design, test, iterate
Wireframes to high-fidelity prototype, tested with real users. I iterate based on evidence, not assumption — and I document every decision so the reasoning travels with the work into development.
